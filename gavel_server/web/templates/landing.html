How it works

One question per table: better or worse?

01

Scan & judge

Judges scan the event QR code, type their name and get sent to their first table. Nothing to install.

02

Compare, don’t score

After each table: “Was this better than the last one?” Humans are consistent at comparisons and terrible at 1–10 scales.

03

Watch it converge

Crowd‑BT turns every vote into a posterior over quality, sends judges where the ranking is least certain, and down‑weights unreliable judges automatically.

Why pairwise

A 7 from a harsh judge beats a 9 from a generous one.

Rubric scores are uncalibrated, so projects live or die by which judge they drew. Pairwise comparisons cancel judge bias, and the active-learning assignment means every vote is spent where it reduces uncertainty the most. Gavel even tells you when the podium is settled.
